# Career Development Experience: Choose Your Own Adventure 01

## Chosen Adventure – Professional networking with military tech‑recruiters

During the Spring 2026 semester at Washington State University, I explored a career path outside of the conventional civilian technology sector by meeting with officer recruiters from the United States Navy.  My goal was to learn about technology‑focused officer roles – particularly the Information Professional (IP) and Information Warfare specialities – which lead technical teams in areas such as cyberspace defensive operations, secure communications, network management and knowledge management.  These roles align with my interest in cybersecurity, network engineering and leadership.

## Career goals

**Short‑term:** complete my computer‑science degree and expand my skills in cybersecurity and networking.  In the next year I plan to earn CompTIA Security+ certification and complete advanced courses in network and systems administration.  I also want to build my portfolio through internships or research projects in network security.

**Long‑term:** I am considering service as a commissioned officer in the Navy’s Information Security because it offers the chance to lead teams defending critical networks and coordinating operations.  Alternatively, I may pursue a civilian career in cyber‑defense or systems engineering while supporting the public sector.  My career goals emphasise continuous learning, technical mastery and ethical leadership.
